The problem

A folder of photos does not show which required evidence is missing.

Teams need checklist coverage, recency, and phase order before a human opens the images. Claiming to inspect the pixels would answer a different question.

Honest limitations

Presence is not workmanship, safety, or compliance.

  • The pipeline never opens or interprets image pixels.
  • It trusts timestamps, GPS, and human-authored tags.
  • A mistagged photo can produce a false coverage result.
  • A qualified inspector must still examine the photos and the site.
