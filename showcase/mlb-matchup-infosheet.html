The problem

Head-to-head history often looks precise because its denominator is hidden.

Season form, venue splits, pitcher-hand splits, and one-pitcher history answer different descriptive questions. Combining them without sample sizes turns a handful of at-bats into a false trend.

Honest limitations

This is historical information, not a projection or betting edge.

  • All players, teams, and games are synthetic.
  • The trend arrow compares measured past averages and does not forecast.
  • Past splits omit injuries, lineup changes, weather, and ballpark context.
  • No profit, expected value, or future-outcome claim is made.
