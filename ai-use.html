How I use them

  • I use AI coding and writing assistants to draft code, tests and text, and to search and summarize documentation.
  • Every measured result on this site and in a report comes from a run I can reproduce, not from a model's answer.

Your material

  • I do not put your code, data, documents or credentials into an AI tool without your written permission.
  • If you allow it, we agree in writing which tool, which material, and whether the provider may keep or train on it.

Human review

  • I read and check everything an AI tool produces before it reaches you: code is run and tested, claims are checked against evidence.
  • I am responsible for what I deliver, whichever tool helped write it.
